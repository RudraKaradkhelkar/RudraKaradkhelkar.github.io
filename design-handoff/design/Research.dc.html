QUESTION

Which assets in a power grid or a transit network matter most for cyber risk? Standard centrality metrics score nodes or edges, one at a time.

APPROACH

Derive new centrality metrics that weigh node and edge importance together, in two dimensions, which the literature doesn't have yet. Then test them on real network topologies from a CAMS partner organization.
